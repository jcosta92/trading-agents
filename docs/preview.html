Grid bot on BTC/EUR (Pionex / Binance / Bybit spot grid) · 15m candles

The spot grid is the most widely used bot type on Pionex, Binance and Bybit, and the first item in most "best crypto bot" lists (e.g. gainium.io/best/grid, bingx.com "Top 10 crypto trading bots of 2026"). It profits from price oscillation inside a range. 

SETUP  16 geometric grid levels from -8% to +8% around the starting price (~1% apart);
       equal EUR amount per level; buys the BTC needed for the sell levels above the price
BUY    resting limit order at each level below the price
SELL   every filled buy gets a resting limit sell one level higher (+~1%)
RESET  if the price stays outside the range for 24 hours, sell everything and rebuild the
       grid around the current price (a "trailing" grid)

DCA bot with safety orders on ETH/EUR (3Commas-style) · 15m candles

The DCA bot with safety orders is the signature bot of 3Commas and is offered by Pionex, Bitsgap and Gainium (gainium.io/best/dca). Settings below are the common community template: 1.5% take-profit, safety orders 1.5% apart with volume scale 1.5 and step scale 1.2. 

BUY    base order of EUR 70 at market as soon as no deal is open ("start ASAP")
BUY    up to 5 safety orders below the entry: first at -1.5%, each next step 1.2x wider,
       each order 1.5x larger (70, 70, 105, 158, 236, 354 EUR; covers a ~11% drop)
SELL   take-profit limit order at +1.5% above the average entry price, moved after every
       safety order; when it fills, a new deal starts on the next candle
STOP   none (classic template)

BinHV45 Bollinger mean reversion on 8 coins (Freqtrade community strategy) · 15m candles

BinHV45 is one of the most copied strategies in the Freqtrade community repositories (e.g. github.com/freqtrade/freqtrade-strategies and many forks). Rules and hyper-parameters are taken verbatim from the published file; 15-minute candles as in the original. 

BUY    the candle closes below the previous lower Bollinger band (40, 2), the band is wide
       (mid-lower > 0.7% of price), the close moved > 1.7% of price, the lower wick is small
       (< 25% of the band width) and the close is not above the previous close
SELL   take-profit limit +1.25% above entry (Freqtrade minimal_roi)
STOP   -5% stop-loss
SIZE   up to 3 open trades (Freqtrade max_open_trades), one third of equity each, at most one
       per coin; scans 8 liquid EUR pairs as Freqtrade setups usually run on a pair list

Adapted here for a EUR 1,000 cash account: long only, no leverage, 5 names. 

SELECT at 09:35 New York time, the 5 names (of the 100 most liquid S&P 500 stocks) with the
       highest volume in the first 5 minutes relative to their 14-day average, whose first
       candle closed up
BUY    stop order at the high of the first 5-minute candle
STOP   10% of the 14-day ATR below the entry
SELL   everything at the close of the session (no overnight positions)

Rebalancing bot, equal-weight BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP (Pionex / Shrimpy style) · 15m candles

Rebalancing bots (Pionex "Rebalancing Bot", KuCoin "Smart Rebalance", Shrimpy) keep fixed target weights and sell what has risen to buy what has fallen. Pionex rebalances when a coin's weight drifts past a threshold; its help page example is a 10% weight rebalanced at 10.25% or 9.75%, i.e. 2.5% of the target weight, used here. 

SETUP  25% in each of BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP (1% kept in cash for fees)
REBAL  when any coin's weight is more than 2.5% away from its target (e.g. above 25.37% or below
       24.13%), sell the overweight coins and buy the underweight ones back to the targets
