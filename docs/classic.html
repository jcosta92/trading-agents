Trading rules (pre-registered)

A1 ETF trend: SMA 50/200 + 12-month momentum, ATR trailing stop (IBKR)
BUY  when close > SMA(50) > SMA(200), the 12-month return (ROC 252) > 0, and the close is a new
     20-day closing high (entry trigger; also prevents instant re-entry after a stop-out)
SELL when SMA(50) < SMA(200)
STOP chandelier trailing stop: highest close since entry - 3 x ATR(20), checked hourly
SIZE risk 1% of equity between entry and stop, max 30% of equity per ETF, whole shares
A1b Same ETF trend rules, Trading 212 cost model
same as A1
A2 Stock factor ranking + SMA(200) filter, ATR trailing stop (US large caps, IBKR)
BUY  monthly: top-10 composite rank (momentum + value + quality z-scores) AND close > SMA(200)
SELL monthly: a holding drops out of the top 25% of the ranking
STOP chandelier trailing stop: highest close since entry - 3 x ATR(20), checked hourly
SIZE equal weight, 10 names, fractional shares
A3 Crypto breakout: Donchian 20/10 with 2N stop (BTC/ETH perps, Kraken EEA, 1x)
BUY  when the daily close breaks above the highest high of the previous 20 days
SELL when the daily close breaks below the lowest low of the previous 10 days
STOP 2 x ATR(20) below entry ("2N"), checked hourly
SIZE risk 1% of equity per N (so a stop costs ~2%), max 50% of equity per coin, total <= 1x
A7 RSI(2) mean reversion on the S&P 500 UCITS ETF (Trading 212)
BUY  when close > SMA(200) and RSI(2) < 10
SELL when the daily close > SMA(5)
TAKE-PROFIT resting at the 5-day average while that average is above the entry price, checked hourly
STOP 3 x ATR(10) below entry, checked hourly; TIME EXIT after 10 business days
SIZE 95% of equity, fractional shares
A4 Funding carry: long spot / short perp when funding > 12%/yr (Bitvavo + Kraken)
BUY  spot + SELL the same quantity of perp when the 7-day average funding > 12%/yr
EXIT both legs when the 7-day average < 4%/yr or the last 24h average funding is negative
COLLATERAL if the perp account goes above 2x, sell spot and buy back perp to return to 1x
A5 Put credit spreads on XSP: VIX filter, 50% take-profit, 2x stop (IBKR)
SELL a ~16-delta XSP put 30-45 days out and BUY a put ~0.5% of the index lower, at most weekly,
only when 13 <= VIX <= its 1-year 95th percentile
TAKE-PROFIT when the spread can be bought back for <= 50% of the credit (checked hourly)
STOP-LOSS when it costs >= 2x the credit, or the index trades at/below the short strike (hourly)
TIME EXIT at 21 days to expiry
SIZE total max loss of open spreads <= 35% of equity
A6 LLM news: Claude-labelled headlines, 7% stop / 10% target / 5-day exit (IBKR)
BUY  up to 3 names whose headline is labelled materiality >= 2 and direction +1, with a
     positive 1-month return; one third of equity each
STOP 7% below entry; TAKE-PROFIT 10% above entry; TIME EXIT after 5 business days (hourly checks)
